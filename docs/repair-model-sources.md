# Repair model directory sources

Checked 8 October 2026.

- Apple identification history: https://support.apple.com/en-gb/108044 — covers the original iPhone through the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max. Existing SE year-based labels/URLs are retained. No unverified standard iPhone 18 or 18 Plus entry is generated.
- Apple UK phone catalogue: https://www.apple.com/uk/iphone/ — lists iPhone Duo, iPhone Air and the current phone range.
- Samsung UK phones: https://www.samsung.com/uk/smartphones/ — current S26/FE and Fold8/Flip8 range.
- Samsung UK A-series guidance: https://www.samsung.com/uk/mobile-phone-buying-guide/best-samsung-a-series-phone/ — current A57/A37 and A56/A36/A26/A17.
- Samsung launch confirmation: https://news.samsung.com/uk/samsung-galaxy-a57-5g-and-galaxy-a37-5g-now-available
- Samsung model references: https://news.samsung.com/uk/meet-samsung-galaxy-s25-fe-the-gateway-to-the-galaxy-ai-and-flagship-essentials — S25/FE/Edge and older flagship/foldable model names.
- Google phone history and current models: https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/7158570?hl=en — Pixel family including Pixel 11 and Pixel 10a.

Galaxy coverage focuses on S, A, Note and Fold/Flip families. It does not claim to enumerate every regional, carrier or enterprise SKU. Include the exact 4G/5G variant where known in an enquiry; unlisted devices can still use the general form. None of these manufacturer sources establishes iTop repair support or stock. No manufacturer specification, warranty, pricing or imagery is copied into model pages.
